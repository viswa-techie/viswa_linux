Below is a **complete beginner → advanced Table of Contents (TOC)** for **Process Management & Scheduling in the Linux Kernel**.
It is structured similar to a **full course + kernel developer reference**, covering **history, hardware interaction, kernel mechanisms, scheduling algorithms, synchronization, debugging, and performance**.

This subsystem is important because **every program running in Linux is a process or thread**, and the **scheduler decides which task runs on the CPU**.
